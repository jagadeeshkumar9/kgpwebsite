Smart devices & their uses

Field devices built for real infrastructure

Every device reports into the same console — the same alerts, the same history charts, the same remote-control model — whatever it's monitoring.

💧

Smart Water Tank Indicator

Monitor water level and volume; operate pumps remotely; support automatic/manual modes and restart control.

🗑

Smart Dustbin

Monitor waste fill level, temperature, gas and odor conditions to support efficient waste collection.

🚗

Smart Car Parking

Monitor occupancy, free/occupied slots and total capacity; support remote gate operation.

💧

Water Quality Monitor

Monitor pH, TDS and turbidity; identify warning conditions and support valve/calibration operations.

🌱

Soil Moisture & Irrigation

Monitor soil moisture, air temperature and humidity; control irrigation pumps with auto/manual modes.

💡

Smart Street Lighting

Smart street-light infrastructure integrated into the wider IoT operations concept for centralized monitoring and control.
